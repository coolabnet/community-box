# Sosuite (Soverin Suite): Status Note

> **Beta:** not yet recommended. The product is pre-launch, and its public details are incomplete. Do not plan community workloads on it until the open questions below are answered.

## What the Feedback Proposed

The feedback named Sosuite from Soverin, with Greenhost as a hosting partner. It suggested a shared suite for community groups. Check the original issue for the exact wording and use case. This note covers only what the public pages say.

## What Public Pages Say

Soverin's product page for Soverin Suite ([soverin.com/product/sosuite](https://soverin.com/product/sosuite)) describes these points:

- **Status:** pre-launch. It is not yet available. You can sign up for early access.
- **Launch date:** the page gives two dates. One says summer 2026 and another says late 2026. Summer 2026 has already passed as of this note, so ask Soverin which date is current.
- **Scope:** described as a privacy-focused platform for email, calendars, contacts, documents, file storage, video calls, and chat. It also lists single sign-on, an API, and identity management. It is aimed at freelancers, startups, and small and medium businesses.
- **Hosting:** powered by Nextcloud and hosted by Greenhost. The page says the servers are in the Netherlands on Soverin's own hardware.
- **Price:** not given on the page.
- **Platforms:** the page does not list apps or platforms. It shows a browser dashboard in its image.

Soverin's home page ([soverin.com](https://soverin.com/)) links to Soverin Suite in its menu. It does not describe it. Its live products are a private mailbox and custom domains. Its listed price for the mailbox is 3.25 euros per month, billed yearly. That price is for the mailbox product, not for Sosuite.

The [Greenhost home page](https://greenhost.net/) describes virtual servers, sustainable web hosting, and managed services. It does not mention Sosuite or Soverin. So the partnership is confirmed only by the Soverin product page.

## Name Clash

Another company runs a product called SoSuite. That product is a customer relationship management tool. The page at [sosuite.com](https://www.sosuite.com/) says it is a division of SaaS Ventures, LLC. It is not the Soverin product. Check the company name before you search or sign up.

## What Is Not Known

- Whether Sosuite can be self-hosted on a community box. Not stated.
- Data export, backup, and migration steps. Not stated.
- Service level, support hours, and uptime targets. Not stated.
- Offline use or local network access. Not stated. A cloud suite needs internet by design. (inferred)
- Pricing for community groups or nonprofits. Not stated.

## Open Questions

Ask the partner these questions before any recommendation:

1. Which launch date is current: summer or late 2026?
2. Who operates the service day to day: Soverin, Greenhost, or both?
3. Is there a self-hosted option, or a way to run it on a community server?
4. What is the price, and is there a discount for nonprofits or community networks?
5. Which apps and platforms will be supported at launch?
6. How can data be exported, and in what format?
7. What backup and restore process exists, and how long does it take?
8. What is the uptime target and the support process?
9. Where is the data stored, and what data processing agreement is offered?
10. Is the beta open to community groups, and what is the feedback process?

## Recommendation

Not yet recommended. Revisit this note after Soverin confirms the launch date and answers the questions above. Test with a non-critical account first.
