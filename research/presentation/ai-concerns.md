# 5.4 Concerns

- **Privacy.** Some online AI services may use what you type to improve their models. This depends on the provider's policy and settings. Do not send sensitive community data, such as member lists or health details, to a free service. Read the terms first.
- **Cost.** Good AI services often cost money, and prices can rise. A community that depends on one service may pay more later. Plan a budget, or use tools that run on the Community Box.
- **Data sovereignty.** Sovereignty means the community controls its own information. When data is stored on another company's servers, the community may lose that control. Keeping key records on local hardware helps.
- **Misinformation.** AI tools can write answers that sound right but are wrong. A community member who trusts them without checking may act on bad advice. Check important answers against a trusted source.
- **Information paradigm shift.** People now get answers from a chat tool instead of searching, reading, or asking a neighbor. This changes how people learn and who they trust. Communities should talk about this change openly.
